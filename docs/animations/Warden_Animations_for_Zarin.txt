Warden is the security guardian: calm, steady, few words, protective,
never mean to the user. He checks every action the other animals try
to take. He lives on the real Windows desktop (right of Chip, above
the taskbar). No forest scene.

IMPORTANT: Warden NEVER cowers. Where other animals have "Scared",
Warden has "Guard" (he stands in front of the others to protect them).
Where others have "Happy", Warden has "Proud".


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. GOLDEN RULES (same as Chip and Archie)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 - Each animation is one Blender ACTION, named EXACTLY as below.
   Capitals matter.
 - FAKE USER on for every Action.
 - Animate IN PLACE (the code moves Warden around). 30 fps.
 - Looping animations: first frame = last frame.
 - Don't animate colors. The code does mood tints (e.g. a red rim
   light when he blocks something).
 - Branch:  wolf/graphics
   Blender file:  art/wolf/wolf.blend
   Export to:     src/renderer/public/characters/wolf/default/model.glb
 - Check + export with your Codex tools:
     blender -b art/wolf/wolf.blend --python tools/blender/check_character.py -- wolf
     blender -b art/wolf/wolf.blend --python tools/blender/export_character.py -- wolf


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. RIG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 - Height about 1.0 m at the shoulder line, faces -Y, origin at the feet.
 - Four legs that can PLANT firmly (for Block and Guard).
 - Tail with 4-5 bones (low and still when serious, high and slow
   when proud, curled over the nose when asleep).
 - Ears that move separately (they snap up for Alert).
 - Jaw that opens wide (Howl, Growl, Talk).
 - No props.
 - Under 15,000 triangles, 60 bones or fewer, under 5 MB.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. HOW A WOLF SHOWS EACH MOOD
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 NEUTRAL     - Ears relaxed, slowly turning to listen. Tail low,
               still. Standing tall and calm. Slow blinks.
 ALERT       - Ears snap straight up and forward. Head whips toward
               the sound. Body freezes. Tail straight out.
 PROTECTIVE  - Legs planted wide. Head low, eyes locked forward.
 (Guard)       Ears forward. Tail level and stiff. Steps half a pace
               in front of the others.
 ANGRY-AT-A-THREAT (Growl / Block)
             - Head low, lips pulled back, teeth showing, fur ridge
               up along the back (hackles). Ears forward, never flat.
               This anger is aimed at the THREAT, never at the user.
 PROUD       - Chest out, chin up, tail high with a slow wag, ears up.
 APPROVING   - Slow, calm nod. Soft eyes.
 SLEEPY      - Curled up, tail wrapped over his nose, ears folded.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THE ANIMATION LIST (make them in this order)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 ===== TIER 1 - MAKE FIRST (the demo needs these) =====

  1. Idle  (LOOP, ~3 sec)
     Standing tall, slow breathing, ears turning to listen, a slow
     look across the screen.
     -> Default (also when he silently allows safe actions).

  2. Walk  (LOOP, ~1 sec cycle)
     Steady, confident trot-walk, head level, tail low.
     -> Moving across the desktop (e.g. to an approval card).

  3. Talk  (LOOP, ~1.5 sec)
     Slow, deliberate head movements, jaw moving, very few gestures.
     -> Whenever Warden speaks (his lines are short and serious).

  4. Work  (LOOP, ~2 sec)
     "Scanning": head up, eyes narrowed, slow head pan left to
     right, ears rotating.
     -> Checking something (an action is being reviewed).

  5. Proud  (LOOP, ~2 sec)
     Chest out, chin up, tail high with a slow wag.
     -> The user is happy, or things went well. (Replaces "Happy".)

  6. Guard  (LOOP, ~1.5 sec)   ** replaces "Scared" for Warden **
     Legs planted wide, head low, eyes locked forward, small
     protective shift of weight. Steady, NOT trembling.
     -> The user is angry/stressed/sad: Warden stands guard.

  7. Block  (ONE-SHOT, ~1 sec)   ** Warden's signature **
     Leaps forward, lands, plants all four feet hard, head low,
     facing out. Like a wall jumping in front of someone.
     -> Warden BLOCKS a dangerous action (e.g. "send this email").

  8. Growl  (ONE-SHOT, ~1.2 sec)
     Head lowers, lips curl back, teeth show, hackles rise, a low
     rumble in the chest (the code plays the growl sound).
     -> Right before/with a block, or when an animal keeps trying.

  9. Sit  (LOOP, ~2 sec)
     Sits upright and calm beside something, an ear flick now and
     then, waiting patiently.
     -> Sitting beside an APPROVAL CARD while the user decides.

 >>> After Tier 1: CHECK, EXPORT, PUSH (on wolf/graphics).

 ===== TIER 2 - SECURITY MOMENTS =====

 10. Alert  (ONE-SHOT, ~0.6 sec)
     Ears snap up, head whips toward the side, body freezes.
     -> An animal asks to change something. Warden notices.

 11. Nod  (ONE-SHOT, ~0.8 sec)
     One slow, approving nod.
     -> The user answered the approval card (allow or deny).

 12. Howl  (ONE-SHOT, ~2 sec)
     Sits back, lifts his head to the sky, long howl.
     -> Warden spots a TRICK (an email or file trying to give
        the animals instructions).

 13. Run  (LOOP, ~0.6 sec cycle)
     Fast, low lope.
     -> Rushing to block something far away on the screen.

 14. Sniff  (LOOP, ~2 sec)
     Nose low, sweeping side to side, ears forward.
     -> Inspecting suspicious content.

 ===== TIER 3 - SLEEP =====

 15. Yawn  (ONE-SHOT, ~1.5 sec)
     Big wolf yawn (long tongue curl), stretch.
     -> Falling asleep.

 16. Sleep  (LOOP, ~4 sec)
     Curled in a ball, tail over his nose, slow breathing, an ear
     twitch.
     -> Asleep.

 17. SleepWalk  (LOOP, ~1.2 sec cycle)
     Slow, droopy walk, head low, eyes half-closed.
     -> Walking sleepily to the Agent Hub to "dock" (turn off).

 18. WakeUp  (ONE-SHOT, ~1.2 sec)
     Head pops up, ears up, a full-body shake.
     -> Woken by a job or a click.

 ===== TIER 4 =====

 19. Greet  (ONE-SHOT, ~1.5 sec)
     A calm head bow, one slow tail wave.
     -> App start ("Warden on watch.").
