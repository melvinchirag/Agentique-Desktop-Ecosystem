Pixel handles your email, calendar and documents: sorts letters,
writes drafts, files reports. Crisp, clever, upbeat, tidy, a little
techy. Lives on the real Windows desktop (left of Chip, above the
taskbar).

GRAPHICS MUST BE FAST: only 6 animations are needed. The app makes
everything else (scared, sad, sleep, yawn, greet, ...) in code.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. CHARACTER BRIEF (you decide the look)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 - A CUSTOM character, not an animal (robot, living envelope, paper
   creature, sprite... your call). Accent color #7A6A9E (purple).
 - Must read at about 70-90 px tall next to the animals: bold
   silhouette, big hands, high contrast.
 - Needs two hands that can hold a letter and a pen, plus a face for
   eyes and a mouth (MouthOpen + Blink shape keys if you have time).
 - If it floats, still make Walk (a hover-glide) with the base at the
   origin. About 0.7 m tall, faces -Y, origin at the base.
 - Props on a hand bone: Prop_Letter, Prop_Pen (Prop_Stamp optional).
 - FIRST post 2-3 concept images to Chanuth. He picks one, then rig.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. GOLDEN RULES (same as the others)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

 - One Blender ACTION per animation, named EXACTLY as below.
 - FAKE USER on for every Action. Animate IN PLACE. 30 fps.
 - Loops: first frame = last frame. No color animation.
 - Branch:  pixel/graphics
   Blender file:  art/workspace/workspace.blend
   Export to:     src/renderer/public/characters/workspace/default/model.glb
 - Check + export with your Codex tools:
     blender -b art/workspace/workspace.blend --python tools/blender/check_character.py -- workspace
     blender -b art/workspace/workspace.blend --python tools/blender/export_character.py -- workspace


---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. THE 6 ANIMATIONS (this is everything)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Idle  (LOOP, ~3 sec)    Breathing/bobbing, a look around, a blink.
  2. Walk  (LOOP, ~1 sec)    Walk or hover-glide, in place.
  3. Talk  (LOOP, ~1.5 sec)  Head bobs, small hand gestures, mouth moves.
  4. Happy (ONE-SHOT, ~1.2)  Hop, little spin, grin.
  5. Write (LOOP, ~2 sec)    ** Pixel's signature ** Writes on a letter
                             with the pen. Plays for ALL Google work.
  6. Handoff (ONE-SHOT, 1.2) Holds the envelope out with both hands,
                             small nod. (Giving a draft/file to Chip.)

 >>> Then: CHECK, EXPORT, PUSH (git add / commit / push on pixel/graphics).
